# study brew

**A bilingual (EN / 中文) map of the cafés I've actually sat in, across the three cities that shaped me.**

Hangzhou is home. Ann Arbor is where I went to college. New York is where work and life happen now. In every one of them, my favorite thing has been finding a café worth a whole afternoon.

## Why I built it

Reviews tell you how the latte tastes. They don't tell you whether you can sit and think there for three hours. I wanted one place for the cafés I'd actually go back to, organized the way I lived them.

## What it does

- **A globe you can spin.** Hangzhou, Ann Arbor and New York sit on one dotted route, in the order I lived in them.
- **A ring of photos around each city.** Tap any photo to enlarge it, then swipe through the rest.
- **A street map for each city.** Numbered pins joined by a route; scroll the list and the map flies to each café.
- **English / 中文 toggle**, and one tap to open any café in Google Maps or Amap.

## How I built it

- Designed and built with AI-assisted coding (Claude), iterating from references I collected
- Café list curated in Notion from my own visits; photos are my own
- d3-geo for the globe, Leaflet with OpenStreetMap / CARTO tiles for the city maps, Natural Earth land data
